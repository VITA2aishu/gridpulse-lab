# Adoption and independent use

GridPulse Lab welcomes public reports from people who use the project in a
class, experiment, article, prototype, demonstration, or monitoring comparison.
Reports help maintainers understand where the lab works, where it fails, and
which scenarios should be developed next.

## Report your use

Open an [independent use report](https://github.com/VITA2aishu/gridpulse-lab/issues/new?template=independent-use.yml)
and include:

- the GridPulse Lab version or commit tested;
- the scenario and environment;
- what you evaluated or learned;
- reproducible results, including limitations or failures;
- a public link to an integration, article, course, report, or repository when available.

Submitting a report does not imply endorsement by the project or its maintainer.
Reports must use synthetic or public data and must not contain confidential,
employer, customer, or production information.

## Public uses

No independent uses have been documented yet. Verified reports will be listed
here with the reporter's permission. Entries will link to public evidence and
identify the version used; promotional claims without reproducible details will
not be included.
