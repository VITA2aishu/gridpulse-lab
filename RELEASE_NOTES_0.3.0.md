# GridPulse Lab v0.3.0 — Combined Health and Recovery

GridPulse Lab v0.3.0 combines five telemetry-health signals into an explainable
per-asset assessment and records recovery after unhealthy conditions.

## Highlights

- combined freshness, progression, connectivity, processing lag and data quality;
- health score, status and component signals exposed through the API;
- Prometheus metrics and the local dashboard expose combined health;
- recovery-state tracking records transitions back to healthy operation;
- focused tests cover healthy, degraded, stale, frozen, bad-quality and unavailable scenarios;
- an independent evaluation guide and structured use-report template support reproducible external testing;
- citation metadata makes tagged releases easier to reference in research and technical reports.

All assets and telemetry are fictional or generated. The project contains no
production endpoints, employer data or proprietary operational configuration.
